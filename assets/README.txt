Replace these files with personal media, keeping the same names:
- kuku-photo.svg: your one special photo, or paste a direct image URL into config.photo in script.js
- kuku-memory.mp4: your one special video (or update config.video in script.js)
- background-music.mp3: optional music (or update config.music in script.js)

To personalize the page, edit the config object at the top of script.js. No build step is required.
